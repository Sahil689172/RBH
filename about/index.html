Six Decades of Trust

From a single shop in Gwalior to a legacy that walks with generations

The Beginning

In 1959, Shri Sita Ram Poply laid the foundation of Rahi Boot House with a simple vision — to provide quality footwear and honest service to the people of Gwalior. What began as a modest footwear shop soon became a trusted destination for families seeking comfort, durability, and value.

1959
1970

A Milestone Partnership

A major turning point came in 1970 when Rahi Boot House became associated with Bata, one of India's most respected footwear brands. This partnership strengthened the store's reputation and gave customers access to high-quality footwear from a nationally trusted name.

As demand grew, so did the trust of the community.

Rising From The Ashes

The year 1984 brought one of the greatest challenges in the store's history. During the period of unrest following the assassination of Prime Minister Indira Gandhi, the shop was completely destroyed by fire.

Years of hard work seemed lost overnight.

But giving up was never an option.

With determination, resilience, and the support of loyal customers, the family rebuilt Rahi Boot House from the ground up. What could have been the end of the story became the beginning of a stronger chapter.

1984
1987

A Legacy Passed Forward

As the business continued to grow, the responsibility of carrying forward its values was passed from the first generation to the second generation of the Poply family.

The transition was built on the same principles that had guided the shop since 1959 — trust, customer service, and commitment to quality. While the faces behind the counter evolved, the values remained unchanged.

Serving a Growing City

Throughout the 1990s, Rahi Boot House expanded its collection to meet the changing needs of customers. From school shoes and sports footwear to men's, women's, and children's collections, the store continued to grow while staying true to its values.

Generations of families began returning year after year, making Rahi Boot House a household name in Gwalior.

1990s
2009

Fifty Years of Trust

In 2009, Rahi Boot House proudly celebrated 50 years of service.

For half a century, the store had served thousands of customers, becoming more than just a footwear shop—it had become a part of countless family memories, school journeys, celebrations, and everyday life.

Recognition and Excellence

Years of dedication were recognized when Rahi Boot House received multiple awards and appreciations from leading footwear brands including Bata, Campus, Lakhani, and several others.

These recognitions reflected the store's commitment to customer satisfaction, retail excellence, and long-standing relationships with some of India's most trusted footwear brands.

2013
2024

65 Years of Legacy

In 2024, Rahi Boot House proudly marked 65 years of serving families across Gwalior.

What started as a small footwear store in 1959 has grown into a trusted destination known for quality, reliability, and personal service. For over six decades, customers have returned generation after generation, making Rahi Boot House an enduring part of the city's story.

Walking With You Since 1959

More than six decades later, Rahi Boot House continues to serve the people of Gwalior with the same values on which it was founded:

Trust. Quality. Service.

Today, the store proudly offers footwear from leading brands including Bata, Campus, Liberty, Paragon, Abros, Red Chief, Lakhani, Spark, and many others.

What began as a small family business has grown into a trusted footwear destination, serving generations of customers while continuing to walk alongside them through every stage of life.
